# Motor 11 — Evidence and source rules

These rules decide what the channel may say. They are not optional and they apply to every claim
in every section of the report.

## Open universe, no institution-based hierarchy

- **Government and official:** BLS, DOL, Census, Fed, BEA, Treasury, IRS, SEC/EDGAR, CFPB, FDIC,
  SBA, FTC, USPTO, NIST, Congress.gov, CBO, CRS, Federal Register and other relevant agencies.
- **Original company material:** filings, releases, product documentation.
- **Research:** studies, working papers, datasets.
- **Credible journalism and specialist reporting.**
- **Search, audience and platform signals** (vidIQ, YouTube) — demand evidence, not fact evidence.

Discover through primary evidence AND credible market reporting; government confirmation is not
mandatory. Record only the signals actually consulted.

## Judging a source

Primary/official ≠ governmental, independent or infallible. Hosting does not certify a filing.
Assess each source on: directness, method, scope, transparency, timeliness, conflicts of interest.

Prefer accessible primary evidence. Otherwise, transparent original reporting may support bounded,
attributed claims ("according to Indeed Hiring Lab..."). State reliance, uncertainty and what was
verified; seek corroboration. **Syndication is not independent confirmation. Reputation is not
proof. Popularity is not proof.**

Keep discovery, evidence and demand separate.

## What counts as verified

- A search-result snippet or a page that could not be opened is a **lead**, not evidence. Find a
  readable source or mark the claim **UNRESOLVED**.
- **Never claim a verification that was not performed.** If a number was not found in the source
  that supposedly contains it, say exactly that.

## Decisive claims need all of this

Source, publication date, data/event period, URL, population, denominator, unit, geography,
statistical meaning. Flag any gap. Prefer dated releases and permalinks; for pages that change,
record the access date.

## Source-to-Claim Fit

Rate each source against each specific claim (not in general):
**DIRECT AUTHORITY / SUPPORTING AUTHORITY / CORROBORATING SOURCE / CONTEXT ONLY.**
A fit rating is about this claim, never a guarantee of truth.

## Statistics

- Separate stocks/flows, net/gross, levels/rates, nominal/real.
- Job openings = point-in-time stock. Hires = flow over the period. Payroll change = net, not
  gross hiring.
- A trend or slowdown requires comparable periods or benchmarks.
- Note revisions to earlier figures when the release reports them.

## Never convert

- correlation → causation
- applications → outcomes
- postings → hiring
- announcements → results
- forecasts → facts
- company or global findings → U.S.-wide claims

Distinguish preliminary from reviewed studies, and proposed from enacted from effective rules.
State limits. Invent nothing.

## Convergence

Test meaningful convergence and divergence between sources; never force agreement. Context is not
causation. **An unsupported decisive claim blocks qualification.**
