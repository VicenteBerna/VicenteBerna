## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Primary objective:** excellence and the greatest possible attraction and retention — truth is
   the condition, never the limit.
2. **Facts:** official, primary, **current** sources; every number verified; never invent.
3. **AI-persona rule (YouTube monetization):** the AI narrator never says "I", has no name, never
   presents as a person or expert; information and reflection, **never financial advice**.
4. **The creator approves** themes, titles, thumbnails, viewer promise and narration. Never write an
   approval that did not happen. Never publish without explicit authorization.
5. **Length:** long videos 10–12 min or more (≥1,400 words of narration); watch hours drive
   monetization.
6. **Secrets** (API keys, passwords, tokens) never go into the conversation or into this repository.
7. **Heavy media** (audio, final videos) stays outside Git, in the creator's `midia/` folder.
