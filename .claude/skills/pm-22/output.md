# Motor 22 — Report and handoff format

Report in **English**, with a **Portuguese summary at the top**, in plain language and simple tables
(one item per row, no jargon).

---

Short: the champion title and why it won; thumbnail text; viewer promise; duration and character
count; voice proposal; credits; what needs approval. Then:

|---|---|---|---|---|

All 6 titles, one per row, ordered; the Decisão column says Campeão / Top 3 / Não escolhido.

## A. EXECUTION

IDs (Project, Source Execution, derived M33 Destination and how), state, Champion approval found
where, sources reopened, freshness re-check result, access limits, vidIQ calls and credits.

## B. KEYWORD & INTENT — PROPOSED

Primary and adjacent intent, keywords, audience language, title–demand intersection, brand fit.
Measured / qualitative / inferred kept separate.

## C. TITLES

The 6 titles, each with: characters (measured), mobile check, repetition check, and the five
qualitative assessments. Top 3. Champion title.

## D. PACKAGE

Title · Thumbnail Promise (concept) · Thumbnail text (≤3 words) · Viewer Promise · why it won ·
boundary/risk.

## E. RETENTION

The nine beats with paragraph numbers and estimated timing; the viewer's question; the journey;
loops and where each pays off; resets; mid-video re-hook; promise-fulfillment point. The 3–5 hook
options with the chosen one and the runner-up.

## F. FULL NUMBERED NARRATION — vN

The complete narration only, numbered paragraphs, no labels inside the text. Then, separately:
word count, character count, 150 wpm assumption, pauses assumed, estimated duration; pronunciation
notes.

## G. VOICE, DESCRIPTION, SHORTS SEEDS

Voice proposal and reason. The full YouTube description (with SOURCE NOTES and a marked chapters
placeholder for M33). The 2–3 Shorts seeds (paragraphs, new first line, estimated length).

## H. SOURCE MAP

| ¶ | Exact wording | Fact ID | Source | Published | Data period | URL | Class | Scope/limits | Verified |

Interpretations labelled. No unrelated citations.

## I. VALIDATION / LOCK SUMMARY

PASS / FAIL / PENDING: inputs, Champion approval, IDs; freshness; intent without invented demand;
six truthful distinct titles; measured lengths; complementary thumbnail; promise fulfilled; hook
rules; caveat placement; max 2 data paragraphs in a row; resets and re-hook; complete natural
script, counts and duration; ElevenLabs rules; sources, classes, limits; narrator type, Preventive
Freedom, single CTA; proposed status and human stop. Fix FAILs. Self-check is not approval, audio
testing or performance proof.

End the completed build exactly with:

**TITLE + THUMBNAIL PROMISE + VIEWER PROMISE + NARRATION LOCK — PROPOSED**

If blocked: report PENDING items and conditions, not a lock proposal.

## M33 HANDOFF (`m33-handoff.md`, only after all four approvals)

Between these exact markers:

    BEGIN COPY-READY MOTOR 33 PROMPT
    END COPY-READY MOTOR 33 PROMPT

Include: M33 PROJECT ID = the DESTINATION EXECUTION ID; SOURCE EXECUTION = the M22 PROJECT ID,
exactly. Approved version and status; brand and audience; the locked Title, Thumbnail Promise and
text, Viewer Promise and full Narration, verbatim; approved voice; paragraph timing estimates;
pronunciation notes; the description (chapters placeholder); Shorts seeds; the full source map;
limits and prohibited claims; retention plan and payoffs; the channel's non-negotiable rules.

Authorize M33 to plan scenes, coverage, timing, production and editing: one dominant idea per
scene, intentional progression and motion, paragraphs need not equal scenes. M33 preserves the
narration and returns any editorial change to M22.

Production policy: optimize profitable watch time, not duration or complexity. Generated video only
when retention or narrative value justifies the cost; otherwise lower-cost treatments that keep
quality and clarity. Video, animated images, graphics, typography, screenshots, documents, charts or
stock follow the approved policy, rights and budget; no expanded stock or web permission. Planning
never authorizes spending or generation: an approved credit limit is required. No invented factual
visuals, no interpretation depicted as a documented event. No placeholders, no "see above".
